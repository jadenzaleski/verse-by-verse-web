Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Verse by Verse is built to work without knowing who you are. There is no account, no login, and no server that stores your data. This policy describes what stays on your device, the one request the app makes to the internet, and what it does not collect.

What stays on your device

Your passages, review schedule, practice history, recitation results, and app settings are stored in a local database on your iPhone. They are not transmitted to the developer or to any third party. If you back up your device, this data is included in your own backup under your control.

Microphone and speech recognition

The recitation activity records your voice and transcribes it so it can be compared with the passage text. Transcription is performed on your device using Apple's speech framework. Recorded audio is not uploaded, and transcripts are used only to grade the recitation you just performed.

These permissions are optional. If you decline them, the recitation activity is unavailable and the rest of the app continues to work.

Bible text requests

When you add a passage, the app requests that passage from a Bible text service operated by the developer. The request contains the translation and the verse reference you asked for. It does not contain an account identifier, a device identifier, or any of your practice data.

As with any internet request, the service receives the IP address the request comes from, and uses it only to apply rate limits that keep the service available. Retrieved passage text is cached on your device so the same passage is not requested repeatedly.

What is not collected

Verse by Verse contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or a profile of what you read or memorize. Nothing you do in the app is sold or shared.

Information Apple may provide

If you have chosen to share crash and usage data with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide aggregate crash reports and download statistics through the App Store. This information comes from Apple, is not linked to you individually, and is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Verse by Verse does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting information that this policy does not describe, this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support page.
