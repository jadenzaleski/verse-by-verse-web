How it works

  1. 1

    Add a passage

    Search by book, chapter, and verse range, in the translation you read. A passage can be a single verse or a whole psalm.

  2. 2

    Practice it

    Type the passage from memory, or turn on the microphone and recite it. Both are graded against the text, word by word.

  3. 3

    Review when it counts

    FSRS-6 estimates when you are about to forget each passage and asks for it on that day, not before.

It listens to you recite.

Saying a verse from memory is a different skill from recognizing it on the page. Verse by Verse transcribes your recitation on your device and marks it against the text: the words you got, the words you missed, the words you added.

The audio is never uploaded. Transcription happens entirely on your iPhone.

The schedule does the remembering.

Each time you practice, FSRS-6 updates how firmly a passage is held and pushes the next review further out. Reviews spread apart as the passage settles in.

Your practice stays on your phone.

There is no account, no login, and no sync server. Your passages, your progress, and your recitations live in local storage on your device. The only thing Verse by Verse fetches from the internet is the Bible text itself.
